Course Description

Artificial intelligence (AI) was born in the middle of the 20th century as a branch of computer science. Early development of the discipline was dominated by the paradigm of symbolic AI, emphasizing reasoning and knowledge representation. However, since the 1990s, significant progresses have been made in the subfield of machine learning. The recent breakthrough in artificial neural network, rebranded as deep learning, represents the state-of-the-art of machine learning in many tasks, including computer vision and natural language processing (NLP). This has in turn triggered an unprecedented enthusiasm and huge investment in AI in the business world as well as in society at large. This course introduces the field of AI to business students with a particular emphasis on deep learning which is driving the current AI revolution. The course consists of 5 modules.

  1. The first module establishes the foundation of AI and deep learning and introduces the main tools for this course.
  2. The second module reviews classical deep learning architectures from the pre-transformer era.
  3. The third module introduces the basic idea and common applications of reinforcement leanring, with a focus on deep reinforcement learning.
  4. The fourth module dives into the transformer architecture which quickly revolutionized the field of NLP since its debut in 2017.
  5. The fifth module introduces large language models (LLM) and techniques for fine-tuning foundation models.

Learning Objectives

The course emphasizes experiential learning and contains many hands-on projects using PyTorch. Specific learning objectives include

  1. Understand the fundamentals of machine learning, including the concepts of overfitting and regularization.
  2. Learn the basics of deep learning, including its origin and basic theory.
  3. Understand the difference between metrics and losses, and learn commonly used loss functions used in deep learning.
  4. Understand the backpropagation algorithm and more recent training techniques such as dropout, batch normalization, layer normalization, and residual learning.
  5. Learn data pipeline
  6. Understand the basic idea and common applications of reinforcement learning.
  7. Learn neural network architectures such as convolutional network, recurrent network, and transformer.
  8. Learn major algorithms of image generation such as variational autoencoder, generative adversarial network, and diffusion models.
  9. Learn the implementation of GPT2
  10. Learn instruction fine-tuning
  11. Learn Quantization
  12. Learn Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)
  13. Learn fine-tuning Foundation Models
  14. Learn the basics of Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF)
