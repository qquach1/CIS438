Course Description

This course introduces the basics of web-based agentic AI applications, with three main objectives. The primary objective is to introduce the design and implementation of agentic AI applications. The second objective is to pave the way for subsequent courses so that students can use what they learn in this course to integrate what they learn in other courses through a web-based user interface. The third objective is to prepare students for the job market by equipping them with a portfolio of projects illustrated as a web-based application.

Learning Objectives

The course emphasizes experiential learning and contains many hands-on projects. Specific learning objectives include
  1. Understand the fundamentals of web technology and web frameworks
  2. Understand the fundamentals of LLM, with a focus on inference
  3. Learn model inference, using both open-weight models and proprietary models
  4. Learn the techniques and applications of Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)
  5. Learn AI orchestration, with a focus on LangChain and LangGraph
  6. Learn the basics of agentic AI
  7. Learn AI reasoning and tool use
  8. Learn commonly used agentic design patterns
  9. Learn Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Relation with other Courses at Simon Business School

This course is related to CIS432 Machine Learning for Business Analytics, CIS433 AI & Deep Learning, CIS 434 Social Media and Text Analytics, GBA478 Introduction to AI & Business, and GBA479 Generative AI and Business Application. Hardware and Software

While subscription to OpenAI or Claude is not strictly required, it is highly recommended, especially if you need to vibe code your projects. A subscription to ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) should be sufficient for this course. I also suggest you create an API key for LLM inference, for example, at OpenAI. Please not that you need to purchase API credit separately (i.e., in addition to your monthly subscription) to make API calls. A $5 credit should be sufficient. Since API credit expire in 12 months, there is no need to purchase more than the minimum (i.e., $5) for the purpose of taking this course. Although not required, you will benefit more if you have access to a laptop or workstation equipped with a decent GPU (e.g., RTX4090).
